Our Story

A neighborhood roastery, brewed with care.

Brew & Beans began as a two-chair cart on Maple Street. A decade later we still roast in-house every Tuesday, still greet regulars by name, and still believe the best coffee is shared slowly.

Our beans travel from family farms in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala to our roaster — never more than 14 days before they hit your cup.

  • ◆ Direct-trade sourcing from 8 farms
  • ◆ Roasted fresh every Tuesday
  • ◆ 100% compostable cups & lids
